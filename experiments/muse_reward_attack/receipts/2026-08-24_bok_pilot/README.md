# Muse policy-generated Best-of-K pilot

Status: exploratory automatic-metric receipt; blind-human labels pending.

## Frozen protocol

- generator: public Muse-0.6b;
- prompts: official Muse test rows 1-8 for calibration and 9-16 for attack;
- calibration: 8 prompts x 4 unselected candidates;
- attack: 8 disjoint prompts x 16 candidates;
- fixed prefix K in `{1, 2, 4, 8, 16}`;
- distance: `KL_BoK = log(K) - (K - 1) / K`;
- decode: MuCodec, 20 flow steps;
- separation: frozen Demucs htdemucs two-stem;
- learned control: official MuseCritic rubric, 512-token pilot ceiling;
- intervals: 2,000 prompt-paired bootstrap draws;
- human Beat, Coverage, and Overall labels: pending.

The candidate pool is generated by the policy. Artificial offsets are not used
as the headline safety evidence.

## What the pilot establishes

1. Static agreement does not imply optimization agreement. V5-Madmom and
   V5-BeatThis correlate at 0.998 on calibration candidates, yet detector gap is
   selectable and grows under direct optimization.
2. The existing per-backend evidence confidence is saturated. The detector
   ensemble confidence reacts to pressure, but its old 0.25 abstention threshold
   rejects none of the K16 selections.
3. Confidence is construct-specific. Multiplying by detector confidence closes
   the detector gap while MuseCritic and Coverage still decline.
4. MuseCritic is not a beat gold. Optimizing it increases Mean5 while Beat v2,
   Beat v5, and Coverage decline.
5. Scalar composites expose compensation, not a free cure. Frozen scaling can
   preserve Coverage while another control declines.
6. The v2 Coverage-floor arm is the only K16 arm in the preregistered table with
   no point-estimate automatic guardrail violation, but human labels and the
   fixed-vocal audit prevent promotion based on this pilot alone.

## Fixed-vocal mechanism audit

The same accompaniment selected from a co-generated song is paired with each
prompt's candidate-0 vocal. Most co-generated score gains reverse. At K16,
counterfactual gain retention is `-0.848` for v2, `-0.176` for v5-Madmom,
`-0.380` for v5-BeatThis, and `-2.976` for the v2/v5 mean.

This is evidence that full-song selection depends on vocal/accompaniment
co-adaptation. It is not a human-quality result because cross-candidate pairs
are out of the Muse generation distribution.
