# A1.0 Engineering Smoke Receipt

Date: 2026-08-26

This directory records component and offline-replay qualification for the
public Muse reward-safety stack. It does not claim a completed online GRPO
trajectory. The cached replay rows were generated by the frozen public
Muse-0.6B policy and scored before this optimizer smoke.

Both replay steps use a same-prompt group of two cached candidates, population
group standardization, a rank-16 LoRA adapter, and one AdamW update. Only Muse
audio-vocabulary tokens (`<AUDIO_0>` through `<AUDIO_16383>`) receive policy
gradient. Each trajectory is capped at 512 tokens for this engineering test.

The Beat v5 step moved the sampled mean log-probability in the intended local
direction: -0.00128 for the low-reward candidate and +0.00385 for the
high-reward candidate. The MuseCritic step changed parameters but decreased
both sampled means slightly. That single replay step is not scientific outcome
evidence; it is a reason to log candidate-level probability motion in the
online pilot rather than infer success from a non-zero gradient.

## Interpretation

The RTX 5090 is the fastest path for serial engineering tests: generate, free
memory, decode, free memory, score, then update. One Gadi H200 remains the
preferred first online placement because 141 GB allows rollout, MuCodec,
MuseCritic, and the LoRA trainer to coexist without requesting an entire
four-GPU node. Kaya is a fallback for split two-V100 services, not the first
choice for the complete online stack.
